Mobile web rules
  • One column, 16px gutters, 44px minimum targets.
  • Primary action always in a sticky footer bar.
  • Sheets, never modals; no hover-only states.
  • Hero image ≤ 420px so copy is above the fold.
  • Photo grids scroll horizontally, never shrink below 210px.
Phase two adds
  • Camera + AR room scan and on-device framing.
  • Push notifications on milestones and payments.
  • Offline site photo albums and PM chat.
  • Apple / Google Pay for milestone payments.
  • Biometric sign-in on the account.
Stays on web only
  • SEO landing pages and paid-campaign entries.
  • Long-form financing terms and legal.
  • Full marketplace catalogue browsing.
  • First-time brief for cold leads — no install cost.
