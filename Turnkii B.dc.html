Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you work on a single apartment, or only whole buildings?+

Both. A single 120 m² apartment is a normal job for us; so is a 90-unit release phased over three quarters.

How is pricing structured?+

Finishing is quoted per square metre against a fixed scope; furniture and FF&E per unit against the style and package you chose. Ranges are shared on the site visit, once we've measured.

Can we keep our own contractor for part of the scope?+

Yes. Many clients keep an existing MEP or joinery supplier. We coordinate them inside our programme and stay accountable for the sequence.

What's covered by the warranty?+

Finishes, joinery and installed systems are covered for a full year after handover.
